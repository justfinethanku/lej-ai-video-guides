Requests is every question a customer has sent, in one list: see what's waiting, reply, and close what's done.

![How a request moves](shot:q02)

## The list

Requests opens on **Open**. Each row is one request: the customer, what they asked, its priority, how long ago it came in, and who has it.

![The list](loop:q03)

- An age in **red** means the request is more than a day old.
- The tabs split requests by where they stand: **Open**, **Waiting on customer** and **Done**. Each tab shows how many it holds.
- Type a customer or a subject in **Search customer or subject** to find one. Search looks at those two only.
- **Priority** narrows the list to Urgent, Normal or Low. **Clear** removes the search and the priority.

## Open a request

Click a row. The request opens on the right: the customer and who wrote, when it came in, its priority, who it's assigned to, and what they wrote.

![A request, open](shot:q06)

To take it on, or hand it to someone else, pick a name in **Assigned to**. The list updates straight away. Press **×** to close the panel.

## Reply

1. Write your reply in the box at the bottom.
2. Press **Send reply**.
3. For 5 seconds a bar at the bottom shows **Undo**. Press it to stop the reply; nothing is sent.
4. After 5 seconds the reply is sent and the request moves to **Waiting on customer**.

![Sending a reply](loop:q08)

> Replies go out from support@relaydesk.example, not your own address.

## Close a request

When a request is finished, open it and press **Close request**, then **Close request** again to confirm. It moves to **Done**.

![Confirming a close](shot:q09)

> Closing can't be undone: a closed request can't be reopened, and the customer can't reply to it. If they write again, it arrives as a new request.
